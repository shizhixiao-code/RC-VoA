# Basis of the two frozen-output sensitivity settings

The settings were recorded in `protocol/PLAN.json` after inspecting the existing provider-diagnosis vocabulary and before calculating their feasibility results. They are exploratory evaluation definitions. They do not change model training, calibration or thresholds.

1. **AK as a non-cancer endpoint (`AK_noncancer`).** The Canadian Dermatology Association describes actinic keratosis as precancerous rather than skin cancer, while emphasizing that it can progress and requires clinical attention. The American Academy of Dermatology likewise describes AK as a precancerous growth. These sources support an alternative binary *malignancy* endpoint that includes explicitly named AK on its negative side. This is not a judgment that AK is clinically harmless or that a referral for AK is inappropriate. The original malignancy analysis excluded AK from binary outcomes while retaining its resource cost.
   - [Canadian Dermatology Association, Actinic Keratoses](https://dermatology.ca/public-patients/diseases-conditions/skin-conditions/actinic-keratoses/), accessed 2026-11-09.
   - [American Academy of Dermatology, Actinic keratosis: Diagnosis and treatment](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/actinic-keratosis-treatment), accessed 2026-11-09.
   - Exact included source labels are `malignant- ak` (MRA), `actinic keratosis` (HIBA/UQ), and `AK` (MCR-SL). No atypical, unspecified or unknown lesion is assigned a binary diagnosis.
2. **Reference-endpoint complete-case population (`endpoint_complete_case`).** This is an explicit analytic alternative to the manuscript's decision to retain endpoint-excluded cases in resource denominators. Only original binary-eligible records remain, and all count-based budgets are recalculated from that population. It tests denominator and cohort-exclusion sensitivity. It is not an unbiased missing-data estimator, a pathology-quality filter, or a correction for selective dermoscopy availability. The rationale comes from the actual dataset's eligibility distinction, not a new clinical diagnosis assumption.

All four upstream metadata files were available. Their SHA-256 hashes matched the original provenance, and their selected original fields matched all 9,168 frozen evaluation rows by unique lesion ID and group. MRA's upstream file contains 922 records, of which the already frozen paired cohort contributes 891. The other upstream files contain 298, 7,756 and 223 records. Original provider-field slices and hashes are retained for portable inspection; this does not re-adjudicate pathology.

The external cohorts contain 92 explicitly named AK lesions and no explicitly named keratoacanthoma. MRA's 55 SCCIS cases already have their own subtype count and a 90% floor; changing the display name to IEC at the same floor would not alter an audit. Ambiguous melanocytic diagnoses, `Other`, and `UNK` remain unassigned in the first alternative and are excluded from the analysis population in the second.
